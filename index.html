Understand

The six big ideas

Read this first. It makes the choices easier.

Risk and returnHigher expected return normally means higher risk. Equity funds sit high; cash funds sit low.
DiversificationSpread money across investments to reduce specific risk. It cannot remove all investment risk.
Bid vs offerUnits are bought at the offer price and valued or cashed at the bid price. The gap is the bid-offer spread.
Protection costsUsually paid by cancelling units. Costs vary with age and cover; charges should be disclosed.
FlexibilityVariable life may allow switching funds, top-ups, premium holidays, withdrawals, and changes in cover.
EthicsTwisting uses misleading comparisons to replace a policy. Rebating offers a special inducement. Both are improper.
Variable life

Values follow the underlying assets. Returns and withdrawal values are generally not guaranteed.

Traditional participating

The insurer smooths returns and may allocate surplus as dividends.

Bonds / fixed income

Best fit for principal protection and a steady income stream, not normally maximum capital appreciation.

Fund roles

Fund managers choose investments; trustees hold assets and protect unit holders.
